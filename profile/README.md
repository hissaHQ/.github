## Hissa Simplifies Equity and ESOP Management

[Hissa](https://www.hissa.com/) helps companies, investors, and employees manage equity in one place. It covers ESOP plans, grants, vesting, and liquidity while helping employees track their holdings and understand their potential value.

### Key Features

- **ESOP management:** Create plans, issue grants, and track vesting.
- **Cap table management:** Maintain ownership records.
- **Equity modelling:** Assess compensation and ownership scenarios.
- **Transaction management:** Manage equity workflows and transactions.
- **Real-time reporting:** Monitor equity and ownership changes.
- **Employee tracking:** View holdings, exercise costs, and estimated value.

### Helping Employees Understand Their Equity

Hissa gives employees a live view of their ESOPs and other equity holdings, including vesting status, exercise costs, and estimated value. This helps them understand their ownership and evaluate potential outcomes.

### Providing Liquidity Through Hissa Fund 1

Hissa Fund 1 is a dedicated ESOP secondaries fund that provides liquidity to employees and other shareholders of growth-stage private companies. Eligible shareholders can explore selling their holdings through secondary transactions, subject to applicable terms and conditions.
